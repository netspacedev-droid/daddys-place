Our Story

A Gampola favourite, since 1989.

Three and a half decades ago, Daddy's Place opened its doors near the old bridge on Nuwara Eliya Road with one simple goal — feed Gampola well. The kottu boards haven't stopped since. What began as a small family kitchen has grown into one of the town's most-loved places to eat, but the recipes, and the welcome, haven't changed.

  • Warm hospitality, every visit
  • Cooked fresh, never rushed
  • Family recipes since 1989
  • A name Gampola trusts
